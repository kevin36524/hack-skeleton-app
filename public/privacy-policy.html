Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

In Short: This application accesses your Yahoo Mail data solely to provide email management services. We do not store, share, or sell your email data to third parties. Your information remains private and under your control.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Hackable Mail ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service to access and manage your Yahoo Mail account.

By using this Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our Application.

2. Information We Access

When you authorize our Application to access your Yahoo Mail account, we request the following permissions via Yahoo's OAuth 2.0 API:

Permission Scope Purpose
mail-r (Read Mail) Read your email messages, folders, and metadata to display them in the application interface
mail-w (Write Mail) Modify your emails (e.g., mark as read/unread, move, delete) based on your actions
openid / profile Access your Yahoo profile information (name, email address) to personalize your experience

What We Access:

  • Email messages and their content
  • Email metadata (sender, recipient, subject, date, folder labels)
  • Yahoo Mail folders and organization
  • Your Yahoo profile information (email address, name)
  • Message threads and conversation history

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information accessed from your Yahoo Mail account exclusively to:

  • Display your emails: Show your messages, threads, and folders in our application interface
  • Perform actions you request: Archive messages, move to folders, mark as read/unread, or delete messages when you explicitly request these actions
  • AI-assisted email management: Optionally use an AI agent to help you triage, summarize, and respond to emails based on your explicit instructions
  • Search functionality: Enable you to search through your emails
  • Provide email management features: Organize and manage your inbox according to your preferences
Important: We access your Yahoo Mail data only when you are actively using the Application. Email content processed by our AI assistant is handled transiently and is not permanently stored on our servers.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 No Permanent Email Storage

We do not store your email content or Yahoo Mail data on our servers. All information is:

  • Fetched directly from Yahoo's Mail API in real-time
  • Processed temporarily in memory for display and AI-assisted features
  • Automatically cleared when you close the application or log out

4.2 Authentication Tokens

To maintain your connection to Yahoo Mail, we handle OAuth 2.0 authentication tokens as follows:

  • Access Tokens: Stored temporarily in your browser's secure session storage
  • Refresh Tokens: Used only to obtain new access tokens when needed
  • Token Security: All tokens are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Token Deletion: Tokens are automatically removed when you log out or revoke access

4.3 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • OAuth 2.0 protocol for secure authentication with Yahoo
  • No logging of email content or sensitive information
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure API endpoints with rate limiting to prevent abuse

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your Yahoo Mail data with any third parties. Your email data is:

  • Never shared with advertisers or marketing companies
  • Never used for analytics or tracking purposes beyond core application functionality
  • Never sold or monetized in any way
  • Only accessed by you through our Application interface

AI Processing

When you use our AI-assisted features, email content may be sent to an AI model (e.g., Claude by Anthropic) to generate summaries or suggested actions. This processing is:

  • Done solely to fulfill your explicit in-app requests
  • Transient — data is not retained by the AI provider beyond the immediate request
  • Subject to the AI provider's own privacy and data handling policies

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information only if required by law, such as in response to:

  • Valid legal processes (subpoenas, court orders)
  • Government requests
  • Protection of our legal rights

However, since we do not store your email data, there is minimal information available for disclosure.

6. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data and can exercise the following rights at any time:

6.1 Revoke Access

You can revoke our Application's access to your Yahoo Mail account at any time by:

  1. Visiting your Yahoo Account Security settings at https://login.yahoo.com/account/security
  2. Navigating to "Connected Apps & Websites" or "Recent Activity"
  3. Finding our Application in the list and selecting "Remove Access"

Once access is revoked, our Application will no longer be able to access your Yahoo Mail data.

6.2 Data Deletion

Since we do not store your email data:

  • No email content needs to be deleted from our systems
  • Authentication tokens are automatically removed when you revoke access
  • All temporary data is cleared from your browser when you log out

6.3 Access and Review

You can review what permissions our Application has by checking the connected apps section of your Yahoo Account. The Application only accesses data when you are actively using it.
